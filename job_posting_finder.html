Job Posting Finder

Companion tool — turns a shortlist of companies and/or titles into a prompt that finds real, current job postings.

This is the companion to the Target Company Prompt Builder, not a replacement. That tool researches companies and ranks them (Suggested Priority Rank). This tool takes a shortlist — ideally your top few by that rank — and asks Claude to actually search for live job postings. Best practice: run the company research first, then paste your highest-priority companies and their Suggested Job Title Keywords in here.
Senior / Executive Search
Off — standard mode. Titles drive the search directly, since mid-level and IC roles are usually posted with recognizable titles.
Why this mode is different: at Director/VP/Partner/Managing Director level, roles are frequently filled through internal promotion, executive search firms, and warm referrals — never posted publicly at all. Searching by your exact title guesses mostly fails here, and near-misses (right company, wrong seniority; right seniority, wrong domain) become the most valuable output, not a fallback. This mode runs a two-stage process: a fast, broad Triage pass across every company checking for any senior-level signal (your pasted titles are ignored for this step — they get reused as hints later), then a deep Deep Dive pass — LinkedIn, the company's own careers site, and Indeed/Glassdoor/ZipRecruiter each searched deliberately, plus a verification fetch on the best lead — run on only the handful of companies worth that effort.

"Exact phrases" (default) searches for the literal title text you pasted — works well for common IC-level titles, but real companies rarely use an exact guessed phrase for senior/leadership roles. "Boolean by category" treats your pasted titles as a strong hint, not a literal string: Claude breaks each company's title(s) down into Seniority (e.g. Director, VP, Managing Director), Domain (e.g. OT, Industrial, ICS), and Function (e.g. Cybersecurity, Cyber Risk), then counts a posting as a match if it satisfies all three categories — even if the exact wording or word order differs from what you pasted. Recommended for Director-level and above searches specifically, where exact-phrase matching tends to miss real, current postings.

3 · Scope

Guidance, not a hard cap. Fewer per combination usually means better-verified results — see the honesty note below.

No toggle here on purpose — every doable source is checked by default: LinkedIn Jobs, Indeed, each company's own careers page, Google Jobs, Glassdoor, and ZipRecruiter. "Checked" isn't the same as "found something" — the report will state plainly, per company, which sources were actually searched and which couldn't be (login-gated, no public listings page, etc.), rather than implying full coverage produced full results.

4 · Output

Results always come back as a downloadable Excel workbook (.xlsx) — this requires the Code execution and file creation setting in Claude (Settings → Capabilities).

Green = posted in the last few days, yellow = getting older, red = near the edge of your recency window — a quick visual read on freshness.

Why this matters: a fabricated job posting is worse than no posting at all — it wastes your time chasing something that doesn't exist. This tool's generated prompt explicitly tells Claude to only report postings it actually finds via live search with a real, working URL, and to report fewer results rather than pad the list. That instruction is built in below and can't be turned off. Also built in, always on: a verify-before-trust step — anything specific enough to drive a real result gets a direct link check before being reported, since search snippets and aggregators often show closed or stale postings as current. If your list includes KPMG, Accenture, or EY, an extra caution note is added automatically, since specific leads for those three have repeatedly turned out to be dead on verification.
